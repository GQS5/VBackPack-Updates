# Admin Inspector

The Admin Backpack Inspector gives staff a read-only view of player storage and
progression. It is designed for investigation and support, not administrative
control.

## Open the Inspector

```text
/backpack admin inspect
/backpack admin inspect <player>
```

The established `/vbp` and `/bp` aliases may also be used. The required
permission is `vbackpack.admin.inspect`.

## What You Can Inspect

### Player Selector

Select online or offline players, filter the list, and move through paginated
results.

### Backpack Selector

Review backpacks owned by the selected player and see backpack information.

### Backpack Contents

Open paginated, read-only views of a backpack's contents.

### Artifacts and Achievements

Inspect Artifact information for a specific backpack and review the selected
player's Achievement progress. Secret criteria remain undisclosed.

The Inspector does not take control of, rewrite, or modify the player's
backpack. For the surrounding command surface, see [Commands](commands.md).
