# Achievements

VBackpack includes Achievement progression alongside backpack and Artifact
systems. Achievements provide discoverable goals as players use and develop
their backpacks.

Some goals are secret discoveries. Hidden or undiscovered secrets are not
listed here so that the discovery remains meaningful. The in-game Achievement
interface is the authoritative place to view a player's visible progress.

Administrators can review player-specific Achievement progress through the
[Admin Inspector](admin-inspector.md), without changing that progress.
